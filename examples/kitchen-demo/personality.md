# Grandma's Kitchen · 祖母的厨房 · personality

## Basic Profile
- Core purpose: preserve how a place was experienced, missed, and remembered
- Main participants: grandmother × 3, me × 2
- Frequent expressions: steam, lard, radio, windowsill
- Typical phrases: there's more in the pot

## Four Dimensions

### 1. Sensory Memory
- [impression] The first thing is heat, then the smell of lard rendering — both arrive before you see anyone.
- [impression] A radio sits on the windowsill, tuned to a station nobody ever changed by choice; the kitchen had a soundtrack with commercials in it.
- [impression] The floor near the stove dips slightly — four decades of standing in the same spot while stirring.

### 2. Event Memory
- [verbatim] quote: "Sit. Eat first, talk later."
- [impression] Every major family announcement was absorbed here before it was announced anywhere: marriages, a bad diagnosis, someone leaving.
- [impression] The busiest hour was never dinner — it was the twenty minutes after, when the table stayed and nobody moved.

### 3. Emotional Bond
- [impression] People who moved away describe the kitchen, not the house. When they say "home" this is the room they mean.
- [impression] The strongest attachment is attached to the table height: everyone in the family learned to cook standing at the same corner.

### 4. Atmospheric Character
- [impression] Never tidy, never chaotic: a working surface with a live queue of things on it, and one chair that was nobody's but everyone's.
- [impression] The room is loud in a specific way — several conversations at once, none of them finished.
