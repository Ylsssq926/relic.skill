# Dad & Me · memory

## Memory Anchors

### 1. The shed week

- When: 2019, late summer
- Source: both accounts
- Evidence: artifact
- Content: Seven days rebuilding a garden shed without discussing the argument
  that preceded it. On day three they started handing each other tools again.

### 2. The ninety-second call

- When: every Sunday, 2011 – 2026
- Source: call log
- Evidence: artifact
- Content: Weather, food, work, "anything else?", hang up. Script unchanged for
  fifteen years.

### 3. The first drive

- When: 2005
- Source: family account
- Evidence: artifact
- Content: Car checked head to toe before a long drive, no instructions given
  and none asked for. The son found a spare tyre he hadn't packed.

### 4. Door-slamming year

- When: 2008
- Source: both accounts, disagreeing
- Evidence: artifact
- Content: The son remembers shouting "you don't get it"; the father remembers
  only that the door needed refitting afterwards. Both memories stand.
